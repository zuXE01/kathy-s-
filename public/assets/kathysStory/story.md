Kathy’s Hub was established by its owner, Marc Alan Ong, as a meaningful tribute to his late sister, Ms. Katherine Joyce Ong, who passed away after battling cancer. Beyond being a family member, Katherine Joyce Ong was known for her involvement in the local fire volunteer community in Parañaque. She was one of the founders of the Filipino-Chinese Fire Volunteers in Parañaque, where she dedicated her time and efforts to serving the community and helping others.

One of Katherine Joyce Ong’s known interests was her love for coffee. Inspired by her passion, Marc Alan Ong established a café that would serve not only as a place where people could enjoy coffee and food but also as a lasting remembrance of his sister. The name “Kathy’s Hub” was derived from Katherine’s name and nickname, allowing her memory and legacy to remain an important part of the establishment.

The concept and design of Kathy’s Hub are also strongly influenced by Katherine Joyce Ong’s involvement in firefighting and volunteer service. The café follows a "firefighting and firefighter-themed concept", incorporating elements associated with the fire service into its overall atmosphere and design. This theme represents Katherine’s dedication to firefighting and community service, making the establishment a reflection of an important part of her life and identity. Rather than functioning solely as a café, Kathy’s Hub uses its theme to preserve and communicate the story of Katherine and her contributions to the community.

In addition to its café operations, Kathy’s Hub features a museum that showcases various personal belongings, memorabilia, and collections associated with Katherine Ong. Among the displays are her personal items and collection of motorcycles, providing visitors with a glimpse into her interests, personality, and life. The museum serves as a space where her memories and legacy can be preserved and shared with the community.

In honor of Ms. Katherine Joyce Ong and her personal battle with cancer, Kathy’s Hub has also extended its efforts beyond its business operations by participating in charitable activities and providing donations to children battling cancer. These initiatives reflect the establishment’s commitment to continuing Katherine’s legacy of compassion and service to others. Through these charitable efforts, Kathy’s Hub aims to provide support and hope to children and families affected by cancer while keeping the memory of Katherine alive.

Thus, Kathy’s Hub represents more than just a café. It serves as a place that combines business, remembrance, community service, charity, and the culture of firefighting. Its firefighter-themed environment, museum, charitable activities, and dedication to preserving Ms. Katherine Joyce Ong’s memory all contribute to the unique identity of the establishment. Through these efforts, Kathy’s Hub continues to honor Katherine’s life, interests, and contributions to the community while transforming her memory into an enduring legacy of service, compassion, and hope.
