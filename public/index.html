Our story.
A name. A memory.
A place to gather.

Kathy’s Hub began with Marc Alan Ong’s wish to honor his late sister, Katherine Joyce Ong, and the things she loved: coffee, community and service.

Remembering Katherine

Katherine was one of the founders of the Filipino-Chinese Fire Volunteers in Parañaque. Her dedication to helping others lives on in the café’s name and firefighter-inspired surroundings.

After Katherine passed away following a battle with cancer, Marc created Kathy’s Hub as a lasting remembrance of his sister. Her love for coffee became part of a place where people could come together over food and a cup of coffee.

A legacy of service

The museum at Kathy’s Hub shares her personal belongings, memorabilia and motorcycle collection, offering visitors a glimpse into her life and interests.

In her memory, Kathy’s Hub has also taken part in charitable activities and donations for children battling cancer, continuing her spirit of compassion and service.
