## Platform

## Users and purpose
Customers browse restaurant products and place authenticated demo orders. Staff and kitchen users manage permitted order stages; owners manage the catalog and members. Event customers compare supplied offers and prepare a reservation inquiry.
## Capabilities and constraints
Mobile-first existing HTML/CSS/JavaScript and Express application. Preserve public menu/prices, server permissions, customer privacy, cart/checkout recovery and sandbox-payment safeguards. The reservation page is public: compare packages first, then prepare an email inquiry to kathyshub08@gmail.com. It does not store requests, hold dates, take deposits, confirm bookings or automatically send email. Customers must send the draft themselves; availability and final quotes require restaurant confirmation.
## Brand commitments
Preserve Kathy's existing cream, navy and burgundy identity. Business details and package claims come from user-supplied materials, not invented content.
